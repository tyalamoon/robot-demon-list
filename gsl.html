                              Global Shitty List Guidelines

                              1. Records and evidence

                              1.1 Every record requires a video of the attempt that:

                              • is at least 480p quality. Minimum FPS: 50 on PC, 30 on mobile;
                              • is uploaded to a public video host (YouTube, Twitch, Medal, etc.) with an open link;
                              • belongs to the account owner and is not a joke or a stolen record;
                              • may only be edited to hide personal data or prohibited content. In this case, the original is provided via link and attached in the Comments field;
                              • if the video contains several attempts or levels, the player must specify timestamps for each level in the video description or in the Comments field. Records without timestamps may be rejected.

                              1.2 Clicks must be clearly audible throughout the entire attempt and recorded with a real microphone. Mods that simulate real clicks are not accepted and are considered cheating.

                              1.3 For Top 300 levels, the following is additionally required:

                              • an unedited raw footage file uploaded to cloud storage with an open link (Google Drive, Yandex Disk, MediaFire, etc., but not YouTube);
                              • preferably, the file should be 3–7 minutes longer than the level;
                              • on PC: two audio tracks, one with the clicks and one with the game sounds;
                              • a CPS counter and a clock (time in Megahack or similar mods) visible on screen throughout the entire attempt;
                              • if a mod menu is used, its timer or cheat indicator must be visible on screen throughout the attempt or on the victory screen. The victory screen option requires that the player reached it during the attempt.

                              1.4 Live play (camera on screen) and stream recordings do not replace raw footage for Top 300 levels. A stream may only be accepted as supplementary evidence.

                              1.5 Moderators may request any additional evidence at any time: FPS or CPS counter, handcam (recording of the hand), unedited raw footage for other levels, stream. Refusal to provide requested evidence usually leads to rejection of the record and, in rare cases, a ban.

                              1.6 Records without the required evidence are rejected before verification.

                              2. Allowed and prohibited methods

                              2.1 Prohibited: programs and methods that change the essence of gameplay

                              • macros, bots, GDBot, and any playback of recorded inputs;
                              • Noclip, No Spikes, Hitbox Multiplier, Ignore Damage in your own copy of the level, and similar tools;
                              • Speedhack, Game Guardian, Frame Stepper, and similar; slowing the game down via Lock Delta or abusing Smooth Fix;
                              • Instant Complete, Jump Hack, and anything that changes physics or constants (Speed, Gravity, Physics Bypass, TPS Bypass). Click Between Frames (CBF) does not fall under this rule;
                              • any changes that make the level easier: replacing music with hints, following another player in Globed, etc.

                              2.2 Prohibited during an attempt

                              • Hide Trigger, No Mirror, camera movement or zoom (Camera Move, Camera Zoom), Show Hitboxes, Show Layout, Show Trajectory;
                              • abuse of Hide Pause Menu, Trail Always On, No Shaders, No Particles, Pause Countdown, Free Window Resize, and other mods that make the level easier;
                              • texture packs that change hitbox readability or the visibility of indicators;
                              • third-party shaders that change graphics during an attempt.

                              2.3 Allowed

                              • cosmetic settings: Mini Cube Icon, No Portal Lightning, RGB Icons, Trail Always Off, High FPS Rotation Fix;
                              • Show Hitboxes on Death, StartPos Switcher, Death Tracker, Quests in Pause;
                              • training tools used outside of an attempt, including Speedhack and Hitbox Multiplier;
                              • RivaTuner, Timer Resolution, FPS Bypass, Zero Delay, Click Between Frames;
                              • key remapping, as long as the number of keys assigned to each action does not increase;
                              • level changes made by the player via Level Edit (bug fixes, Low Detail Mode), as long as they do not make the level noticeably easier. Fixing broken automatic parts and removing Click Between Frames blocks is allowed;
                              • accessibility changes for medical reasons (color blindness, photosensitivity, epilepsy, and similar), agreed with moderation in advance.

                              2.4 Prohibited use of exploits

                              • Pause Buffering;
                              • deliberately slowing the game down via Smooth Fix;
                              • changing the FPS value to manipulate frame alignment.

                              2.5 Does not count as a record (treated as practice or cheating)

                              • Noclip Accuracy 100%;
                              • the first attempt in Practice Mode;
                              • playing in Death Link mode (Globed);
                              • StartPos at 0% (for StartPos Switcher);
                              • exiting during the automatic part of a level or before the end screen, if the cheat indicator is shown only on the end screen;
                              • abuse of repeated pauses during an attempt;
                              • playing in the Level Editor.

                              2.6 Game version

                              Records may be made on any version of the game.

                              2.7 Changes to a level by its creator

                              • a new verifier replaces the old one, and the old one becomes the first in the list of completions;
                              • if the level has become harder, all records are rejected.

                              3. Punishments

                              3.1 Punishments are issued by the owner and co-owner. Without direct evidence of a player cheating, the player cannot be banned; the record is rejected.

                              3.2 If a player cheated accidentally and can prove it, the record is rejected without further action.

                              3.3 For deliberate cheating:

                              • first offense: a 6-month ban;
                              • second offense: a permanent ban (may be lifted only in exceptional cases);
                              • in both cases the player receives the "Former Cheater" badge. It stays on the profile permanently and is visible only to the Exposer team.

                              3.4 If a player confesses before verification, the first ban is reduced to 4 months. The Former Cheater badge is still issued, and a second offense remains a permanent ban.

                              3.5 Creating a second account is prohibited solely during a ban (to evade the ban, to impersonate another person, or for any other reason). The punishment applies to both accounts.

                              3.6 Other cases are considered individually: suspicious activity, mass promotion of cheats, interference with the website (trolling, fake records, etc.).

                              4. Ownership of records and levels

                              4.1 By submitting raw footage, the player allows moderation to review it. Any member of the Exposer team may review it.

                              4.2 A record may be removed:

                              • at the player's request, sent to an administrator or via a Discord ticket;
                              • automatically, if the player's account is blocked or banned for any reason.

                              4.3 Before verification, a player can delete their record in their profile. After a record is accepted, it can be removed only by contacting an administrator.

                              4.4 When a level's position or verifier changes, or when it is removed, the reason is published in the Discord channel #level-list-changelog.

                              5. Verification and notifications

                              5.1 Records are verified by the Exposer team. Verification usually takes several weeks.

                              5.2 When a record is accepted or rejected, the player receives a notification on the website with the reason for the decision.

                              5.3 The status of each record is visible on the player's profile.

                              6. Usernames and profiles

                              6.1 A username must:

                              • be between 2 and 30 characters long;
                              • not contain the characters . # $ [ ] / or control characters;
                              • be unique, case-insensitive;
                              • not contain profanity, insults, racism, threats, or personal data.

                              6.2 A username is changed through the rename function. The previous username is saved as an alias, so bans and records follow the player.

                              6.3 Usernames may be changed without limits on frequency.

                              6.4 The flag reflects the place of residence, citizenship, or national affiliation with a country.

                              7. Changes to the rules

                              7.1 The rules may change. Each change is announced on the website, in official social media, and on Discord, and is recorded with a version number and date.

                              7.2 Questions and suggestions are sent to the list team via Discord.

                              Level Submission Rules

                              1. Gameplay rules

                              1.1 Gameplay changes

                              Changing the structure or the click pattern of a level is allowed only in 3 cases:

                              • the original level has not been beaten by anyone;
                              • CPS is too high in a given section;
                              • nerfing the gameplay requires significant structural changes.

                              Other exceptions are granted case by case, unless a situation is common enough to justify changing the rules.

                              1.2 2-Player levels

                              • Levels using 2-Player mode are allowed only if they are verified with a hand-cam.
                              • If 2-Player mode is used only in short parts of the level (like Grief), a hand-cam is not mandatory.

                              1.3 Copying

                              The level must be a copy of an original level that was not made exclusively by you or a friend for the purpose of turning it into a shitty level.

                              1.4 Buffing

                              Buffing is judged by overall difficulty. If a part is harder overall than in the original, the level is rejected.

                              1.5 Length (Challenge List and Spam List)

                              • Challenge List levels must be shorter than 31 seconds (30 seconds or less).
                              • Spam List levels must be shorter than 46 seconds (45 seconds or less).

                              1.6 Difficulty

                              • Classic: the level must be Easy Demon or harder.
                              • Challenge: the gameplay must be Insane Demon difficulty or harder.
                              • Spam: the gameplay must also be Insane Demon difficulty or harder (focused on high CPS).

                              2. Level requirements

                              2.1 Background

                              Any shade of blue or pure black is required. A background from version 1.0 is recommended.

                              2.2 Ground

                              Any shade of blue or pure black is required. A ground from version 1.0 is recommended.

                              2.3 Middleground

                              Any shade of blue or pure black is required. The level is rejected if the middleground ruins its aesthetics, or if visibility is affected differently than in the original.

                              2.4 Objects

                              • All OBJ / 3DL / L colors must be white.
                              • ALL VISIBLE OBJECTS MUST HAVE THESE ENABLED: Don't Fade, Don't Enter, No Glow.

                              2.5 Other colors

                              Allowed only if absolutely necessary (for example, Limbo Keys).

                              3. Allowed objects

                              3.1 Gameplay blocks

                              • a) All Grid Type objects (image 3.1a). Colored Grid objects must be black, white, grey or the background color, and may only be used as decoration blocks.
                              • 3.1a
                              • b) All Layout objects (image 3.1b).
                              • 3.1b
                              • c) All Spike objects (image 3.1c).
                              • 3.1c
                              • d) Solid black objects, specific colored objects, and the slopes shown in image 3.1d.
                              • 3.1d
                              • e) All objects from the Orbs tab.
                              • f) The specific saws and monsters shown in image 3.1f.
                              • 3.1f
                              • g) Breakable blocks.

                              3.2 Decorative objects

                              These objects must not be used as gameplay and may only be used as decoration.

                              • a) Standard Glow objects may only be black, white or blue (grey icon glow objects: image 3.2a). White and blue glow must be approved by a moderator, because it is allowed only to improve the aesthetics of the level. Submit it in #list-support.
                              • 3.2a
                              • b) Specific disappearing objects (full block invisible objects: image 3.2b).
                              • 3.2b
                              • c) Old Brick and Triangle objects (image 3.2c).
                              • 3.2c
                              • d) All Icons, with unmodified colors (image 3.2d).
                              • 3.2d
                              • e) Particles: colors must be black, white or grey; they must be squares, circles or triangles; and they must contribute to the aesthetics of the level. They do not need to be pre-approved.

                              3.3 Other objects

                              • a) All triggers are allowed except Invert Colors, Hue and Edit Color.
                              • b) Shaders are allowed only if the original level has them and they do not hinder the playability of the level.
                              • c) All objects from the two editor tabs shown in image 3.3c may be used.
                              • 3.3c

                              4. Quality rate reference

                              This is what list moderators use to decide the quality of a level (Featured, Epic, etc.).

                              • Rate: must follow all standard list requirements.
                              • Featured: must be aesthetically complex and/or pleasing to look at. Not every level needs moving objects or effects to get a Featured rate.
                              • Epic: all Featured requirements, and the level must have some kind of effects. There is some room for effects that look unpleasant or messy.
                              • Legendary: all previous requirements. The effects must be unique and must not cause extreme lag, and the decoration must be aesthetically pleasing.
                              • Mythic: all previous requirements. The level must be optimized, effects must not cause lag, and visuals must stay coherent and of standard quality across all parts. A Mythic rate is decided by at least 2 staff members who agree that the level deserves it.
